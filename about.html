Our Story

A family spice counter, now online, run the same way it always was.

The counter regulars trust

Spice Route Kitchen began as a family spice counter, the kind where regulars ask for "the usual" and the shopkeeper knows their heat level by heart. We still work that way, only now the counter is online.

Every order is packed from bulk stock the day it leaves us, ground spices are milled in small weekly batches, and dry fruits are checked kernel by kernel before packing. We would rather sell a smaller range we trust than a long list we cannot stand behind.

Good cooking starts long before the pan gets hot, and we take our part of that seriously.

1. Sourced

Whole spices and dry fruits bought in grades we can stand behind, from suppliers we know.

2. Cleaned

Every batch is cleaned and checked by hand before it goes any further.

3. Ground in small batches

Ground spices and blends are milled in small weekly batches, never in warehouse sized runs.

4. Packed to order

Orders are packed from bulk stock the day they leave us, and every pack carries its packing week.

Fresh is not a slogan here

Most packet spices sit in warehouses for months before they reach a shelf. Ours are cleaned, ground and packed in small weekly batches, and every pack carries its packing week. We never add colour, husk or filler to bulk out a pack. What you pay for is what is in the jar.
