Blends we grind ourselves

A good blend is a recipe in a jar. Ours are mixed from whole spices, ground in small batches and packed the same week, so the aroma hits you the moment you open the lid. Each blend page note tells you exactly which dishes it suits.

We never add colour, husk or filler to bulk out a pack. What you pay for is what is in the jar, and every pack carries its packing week.
